# LockSafe advert: positioning and edit plan

## What the reference ad does (EVA alert reels)
- Vertical 9:16, about 30–45s, cut quickly in time with a moody, building music track.
- Night, warm sodium streetlights, shallow focus and bokeh, 35mm grain, handheld.
- Tension comes from what's out of focus: a hooded figure in the background. Nothing violent is shown.
- Short white captions, lowercase-feeling and calm: "The night is ours." / "Feel safe." / "We hope you never have to use it."
- Ends on the product, then the brand, then a link.

## Where LockSafe can stand out
Research (sources at the bottom):
- **The fear is mainstream.** 49% of UK women feel unsafe walking alone after dark, even on a busy street (ONS). Four in five feel unsafe in a park after dark. Almost 9 in 10 women have felt unsafe walking at night, and 71% have changed their route home (YouGov for Active Travel England, 2025).
- **"Text me when you get home" is already the habit.** LockSafe should be the upgrade to that ritual, not a scary gadget.
- **Competitors' weak spots:** the market leader is criticised for needing you to be logged in before the alarm works, and for menus that are cluttered and slow in a panic. People also worry about privacy, and about tracking being misused by abusive partners.
- **EVA needs a £ device on your keyring.** LockSafe is already on the phone in your hand.

### Recommended positioning
> **One press. Everyone who loves you knows where you are.**

Pillars to make every frame earn:
1. **Speed:** one press or hold, no login and no menus. *(Show the thumb pressing the button, clip 07.)*
2. **Your people, instantly:** trusted contacts see your live location. *(Clips 08 and 09: the map, then someone waking up and grabbing their keys.)*
3. **For everyone, not just "women's safety":** joggers, dog walkers, nights out, kids. This broadens the audience past EVA and Hollie Guard.
4. **Nothing extra to buy or carry:** it's your phone.
5. **Privacy-first** (only if true): "You choose who sees you, and when." This answers the biggest objection.

**Only claim features the app really has.** If LockSafe has a fake call, an audible alarm, police or 24/7 monitoring, or auto-alerts for a missed check-in, add a caption for each one. They're the strongest differentiators.

## Suggested cut (about 30s)
1. 01 (3s), caption: *"You know the feeling."*
2. 03 (2.5s), then 04 (2.5s), then 06 (2s): quick cuts, music builds
3. 02 (2s), then 05 (2.5s): peak. Hard cut to black for 0.5s
4. 07 (3s), caption: *"One press."*
5. 08 (3s), caption: *"Your people see exactly where you are."*
6. 09 (2.5s)
7. 10 (3s), caption: *"Get home. Every time."*
8. End card: LockSafe logo with *"We hope you never have to use it."* plus the App Store and Google Play badges.

**App screens:** AI can't draw your real UI. Over clips 07 and 08, replace or overlay the phone screen with a screen recording of the real LockSafe app (CapCut: Overlay, then Mask, then Rectangle with rounded corners, matched to the phone screen). That's what will make the ad feel real and trustworthy.
